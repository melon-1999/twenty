# 05 — Email & Calendar

OBSERVED (Mail tab + settings) + DOCUMENTED (help pages).

## Email — the "Sales Inbox" — OBSERVED

- A dedicated **Mail / Sales-Posteingang** with folders: Inbox, Drafts, Outbox, Sent, Archive; integrations panel (WhatsApp).
- Marked **"part of your Growth plan"** — email sync is a Growth-tier feature; Lite gets one-way Smart BCC only.
- Feature callouts (OBSERVED on the setup screen): link emails to **deals/leads**, **bulk send** to multiple recipients, **open/click tracking**, **templates**; **AI conversations/summaries** (PREMIUM), **Team Inbox** (PREMIUM), privacy/visibility controls.

## Email capabilities — DOCUMENTED

- **2-way sync** with Gmail/Google Workspace, Outlook/Outlook.com, MS Exchange, Yahoo, iCloud (IMAP/Exchange; no POP3). Retro-sync ~6 months.
- **Smart BCC**: log an email into Pipedrive by BCC'ing a unique address (works even on Lite; feeds the Emails report).
- **Templates** with merge fields; **signatures**; **open + link-click tracking**; **group/bulk email**; **scheduled send**.
- Emails auto-associate to the matching contact/deal by address and appear in the record timeline + a "Focus" draft area.
- **Campaigns** (email marketing add-on) is separate from transactional sales email — drag-drop builder, segmentation, campaign analytics; **not included in any base tier**.

## Calendar — OBSERVED (sync prompts) / DOCUMENTED

- **Calendar sync** (Google, Outlook.com, MS Exchange, Office 365), one- or two-way; syncs the **user's own activities**; a calendar view of activities.
- **Meeting scheduler**: shareable booking pages; contact picks a slot → creates activity + calendar event; video-call links; included from Growth.
- Sync banners appear across Activities/home when disconnected ("Sync calendar in under 2 minutes").

## What's essential vs nice-to-have for a competitor (INFERRED)

**Essential (MUST):**
- **2-way email sync (Gmail + Outlook)** in the base tier, with emails auto-linked to contacts/deals and shown in the timeline. This is table stakes and a Pipedrive pain point (gated to Growth).
- **Calendar sync (Google + Microsoft)** so activities and meetings line up with the user's real calendar.
- **Basic templates + send-from-record**.

**Important (SHOULD):**
- Open/click tracking, meeting scheduler/booking pages, scheduled send, signatures.

**Later / optional:**
- Team shared inbox, AI email summaries/compose, bulk email, full email-marketing Campaigns (a separate product; most SMBs already use a dedicated ESP).

## Opportunity (INFERRED)

Email is where Pipedrive most frustrates entry-tier users (sync gated to Growth; Campaigns unreliable per complaints) and where **AI removes real work**: understand incoming emails, auto-link to the right deal/contact, **auto-create activities/follow-ups**, draft replies, and **update the CRM from email content** (e.g. move a stage, log an outcome). See [16-COMPETITIVE-OPPORTUNITIES.md](16-COMPETITIVE-OPPORTUNITIES.md).

## Twenty mapping (preview)

Twenty has **connected-account email + calendar sync** (Gmail/Google + Microsoft, IMAP/SMTP/CalDAV), message threads linked to records, and the worker-driven sync pipeline — a strong, reusable foundation ([14-TWENTY-REUSE-MAP.md](14-TWENTY-REUSE-MAP.md)). Gaps vs Pipedrive: templates, open/click tracking, meeting-scheduler booking pages, send-tracking UI. Twenty's AI + tools layer is a differentiator for the "email → CRM update" opportunity.
